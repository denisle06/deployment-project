(~30 s) Hi, we are Three Dog. Our app is SmartTunes, a PHP karaoke website: songs, albums, playlists, YouTube playback, record a performance, get a score, leaderboard. We treated the app code as given. Our work is the CI/CD toolchain around it: every commit is built, tested and released to production as a gated canary on AWS, and production is monitored. Quick plan: architecture, tools, pipeline, canary, monitoring, problems, then the live demo.
